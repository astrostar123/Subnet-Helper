      The magic number trick

      The "block size" (increment between subnets) is 256 − last mask octet. For /26 the mask is 255.255.255.192, so blocks are 256 − 192 = 64: subnets start at .0, .64, .128 and .192. Find which block your IP falls in, and you instantly know the network and broadcast addresses.
